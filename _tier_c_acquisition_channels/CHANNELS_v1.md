# FIESTA Acquisition Channels — Decision Brief (2026-05-24)

**Context:** Pick 2 of 5 channels to seed FIESTA MVP (https://fiesta-mvp.fly.dev) with first ~50 real users. Audience = Sri Lankans earning USD/EUR/GBP from offshore clients (engineers, freelancers, returnees).

**Headline regulatory hook (use in every draft):** From 1 April 2025, Sri Lanka's Inland Revenue (Amendment) Act imposes a **15% final tax** on foreign-sourced income remitted via licensed SL banks. **Income NOT remitted through banks → standard progressive rates up to 36%.** First Rs 1.8M is tax-free (Personal Relief). FIESTA's pitch: declare correctly under the new regime, save up to Rs 298,980 vs. the naive default. (Source: SL Sunday Times 2 Mar 2025, bizadvisor.lk, taxadvisor.lk.)

#### Draft A — Intro (long-form, guest-post style)
**Headline:** Returning to Sri Lanka after years in Dubai/Singapore/Australia? Your foreign income just became taxable from day 183.

Two rules changed the landscape for returnees in 2025:

1. **The 183-day rule is being enforced strictly.** Once you spend 183 days in Sri Lanka in a tax year (Apr–Mar), you become a tax resident — and a tax resident is taxed on **worldwide income**, not just SL income. Foreign salary, foreign rental income, foreign investment dividends — all in scope.
2. **The 15% remittance rule.** Foreign-sourced income remitted via an SL bank attracts a flat 15% final tax. Income not remitted (kept offshore, brought in informally) falls under progressive rates up to 36%.

For a returnee with a foreign salary + offshore investment income + maybe a Dubai/Singapore property generating rental, the difference between "filed wrong" and "filed right" can be Rs millions per year.

I built **FIESTA** (https://fiesta-mvp.fly.dev) — a free Sri Lanka–specific calculator that models returnee scenarios. It's MVP, free to use, no sign-up wall on the calculator. If you're inside your first 12 months back or planning the move, worth 5 minutes.

#### Draft B — Feature/case study
Case: Software engineer, 6 years in Singapore (SGD salary + CPF + a rental property), returning to Colombo in mid-2025.

The 183-day clock starts the moment they land. Inside a year, they're a Sri Lankan tax resident with worldwide-income exposure. The Singapore salary they finish out + the Singapore rental + the eventual property sale all enter the SL tax net.

Three structural decisions worth ~Rs 1.5M+ per year combined:

1. Time the entry to delay residency by one full tax year

## Recommendation

**Pick 2: Lanka Developers (Discord + forum) AND Sri Lankan Freelancers FB group.**

These two channels share the highest audience–product fit (both audiences are 90%+ exactly the cohort the April 2025 rule targets), have zero acquisition cost, and produce first signal inside 24–72 hours — critical for MVP iteration speed. Lanka Developers brings the higher-LTV technical buyer (US contract devs at $2K–$8K/mo, exactly the savings-example bracket), while Sri Lankan Freelancers brings volume and signal diversity across Upwork/Fiverr/direct-client segments. The Fiverr group overlaps ~70% with the general freelancers group, so picking both is redundant; IT Twitter is high-variance and better as a third channel once the first two have produced testimonials worth amplifying; Diaspora returnees is the highest per-user value but the longest time-to-signal and demands content-marketing infrastructure FIESTA doesn't have yet — defer to Tier C2 or later.

**Strategic landscape flag:** No specialised SL freelancer tax SaaS appears active in these channels yet. Adjacent players surfaced in search: **WealthyIslander.com** (free calculators, content-led, no filing flow), **taxadvisor.lk** / **bizadvisor.lk** / **taxpro.lk** (traditional advisory firms publishing tax-rule explainers, not SaaS), **countrytaxcalc.com** (calculator + guides). None of these offer a full self-serve filing path under the new 15% regime. FIESTA's window is wide right now but will close once one of the advisory firms wraps a UI around their existing service. **Move on the channels in the next 30 days.**
