# Filing handoff to Lanka.tax (Wave 4 — placeholder)

When your return is ready to file, FIESTA hands off the prepared data
to a Lanka.tax accountant for review and electronic submission to IRD.

Status (2026-05-17): the handoff workflow is in build (Wave 4). For now,
FIESTA's role stops at "ledger ready, evidence pack assembled". Filing
itself remains a Lanka.tax manual process.

What you can do today:
1. Maintain your remittance ledger in FIESTA (Wave 1-2 LIVE).
2. Export the year's remittances as a PDF report (Wave 2.5 — ETA mid-year)
   to send to your Lanka.tax accountant via email.
3. Lanka.tax accountant prepares the return in IRD's RAMIS portal using
   your exported data + your evidence pack (the bank credit advices, the
   foreign tax certificates, etc.).
4. You review the draft return on RAMIS and approve electronic submission.

What the Wave 4 handoff will automate:
- Single-click "Send to Lanka.tax" from the dashboard.
- Lanka.tax accountant receives a fully-populated SF Tax_File__c with all
  remittances pre-attached.
- Two-way status sync: you see "received", "preparing", "ready for review",
  "filed" in your FIESTA dashboard.
- IRD acknowledgement back to FIESTA when the return is filed.

Pricing (Wave 4):
- Lanka.tax filing fee is separate from your FIESTA subscription and is
  set by Lanka.tax (currently LKR 15,000 - 25,000 depending on complexity).
- Quoted before you commit; no surprise charges.

Until Wave 4 is live, contact Lanka.tax directly at tax@lanka.tax or
+94 71 460 0000 to arrange filing assistance.

NOTE for the AI Copilot: questions about "when will my return be filed",
"why hasn't Lanka.tax replied", "I want to switch accountants", "my
filing is delayed" should ALWAYS escalate to a human — these are
relationship-management issues, not knowledge-base questions, and
auto-answers risk eroding the human relationship.
