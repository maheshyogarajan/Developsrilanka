# Public Notice IT/2025/01 — Foreign Income Flat Rate (overview)

Effective from 1 April 2025 (Year of Assessment 2025/2026), service-export
foreign income remitted to a Sri Lankan bank account by a resident individual
is taxed at a flat **15%** rate, separately from the normal IIT slab.

Eligibility (all must hold):
- Recipient is a resident individual for the year of assessment.
- The income is for services personally rendered.
- Remittance is received into an SL bank account in foreign currency.
- The full foreign-currency credit is what's assessed, converted at the
  CBSL middle rate on the credit date.

Excluded from the 15% flat rate (and therefore taxed under the normal slab):
- Foreign income from investments / interest / dividends.
- Income from a permanent establishment outside SL.
- Income earned and retained abroad (the 15% rate is on REMITTED income).

Filing mechanics:
- Reported on the same Personal Income Tax return as other income, in the
  foreign-income schedule.
- Evidence pack per remittance (see evidence_pack_requirements.md) must be
  held by the taxpayer for at least 6 years after the year of assessment.

Penalty exposure if misclassified:
- If income should have been taxed at the slab rate but was filed under 15%,
  the differential plus interest plus a 25% understatement penalty applies.
- If income that qualifies for 15% was filed under the slab, taxpayer can
  amend the return within 4 years and reclaim the overpayment.
