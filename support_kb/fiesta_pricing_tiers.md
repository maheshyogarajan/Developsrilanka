# FIESTA — pricing tiers

FIESTA has three tiers, designed for the three observed user shapes among
SL foreign-income earners.

## Free trial — 30 days

What you get:
- Up to 10 manual remittance entries.
- 1 bank-statement import (PDF or CSV).
- Read-only CBSL rate lookups (no manual override).
- Email support (best-effort).

What's restricted:
- No multi-year ledger comparison.
- No evidence-pack export.
- No Lanka.tax filing handoff (Wave 4).

## Pro — LKR 1,500 / month or LKR 15,000 / year

What you get:
- Unlimited manual remittance entries.
- Unlimited bank-statement imports (capped at 10/day to prevent abuse).
- Manual CBSL rate override (with audit trail).
- Multi-year ledger comparison and YoY trend.
- Evidence-pack PDF export.
- Email + WhatsApp support (24h response).
- Lanka.tax filing handoff (Wave 4 — included, no separate fee for
  the FIESTA → Lanka.tax data transfer; Lanka.tax's filing fee is
  separate per lanka_tax_handoff.md).

This is the right tier for: solo foreign-income earners (freelancers,
remote employees, consultants) with 5+ remittances per year.

## Family — LKR 3,500 / month or LKR 35,000 / year

Everything in Pro, plus:
- Up to 5 user accounts under one Family workspace.
- Shared organization view (spouse + children + parents on one screen).
- Family aggregation for joint planning.
- Priority support (4h response window during business hours).

This is the right tier for: households where multiple members earn
foreign income and want to coordinate (e.g. both spouses freelancing,
or parent + adult child both on overseas contracts).

## How to upgrade

From the dashboard → Account → Upgrade. Stripe checkout in USD; we
charge approximately USD equivalent of the LKR price at the day's rate.

NOTE for the AI Copilot: pricing questions are SAFE to auto-answer
when the question is "what does FIESTA cost" or "what's the difference
between Pro and Family". Escalate for: refund requests, billing
disputes, "I was charged twice", "I can't afford this" — these are
relationship issues, not pricing FAQ.
