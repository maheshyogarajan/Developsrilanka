What is §195?

Inland Revenue Act §195 governs "associated person" transactions — deals between you and a family member, business partner, or yourself (e.g. your company paying you rent for a room in your house).

It is not illegal to have a related-party rental — many legitimate arrangements are exactly this. But IRD requires:

  • The arrangement is disclosed on the return.
  • The rent is at market rate (we sanity-check this for you).
  • The room is used wholly + exclusively + necessarily for business (Inland Revenue Act §6).

We'll generate a rental agreement that satisfies these tests so an auditor can sign off cleanly if they ever review your file.
