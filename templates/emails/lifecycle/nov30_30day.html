Sri Lanka's tax return deadline is November 30. That's 30 days from today.

The return needs your Assets & Liabilities (A&L) declaration alongside the income computation. A&L is the audit anchor — IRD uses it to spot inconsistency between what you owned at year-start and year-end.

What FIESTA needs from you to file:

  • A&L declaration finalised (FIESTA pre-built it from your uploads — just review and confirm).
  • Income computation locked (you saw the number in the calculator — same one flows to the return).
  • Tax already paid by Sep 30 (or, if you missed it, paid now with the late-payment surcharge calculated by FIESTA).
