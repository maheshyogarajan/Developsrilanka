3. Service Description

FIESTA helps Sri Lankan taxpayers calculate, generate documents for, and prepare to file income tax. Specifically, the Platform lets you record foreign-income receipts, document expenses, generate service and rental agreements, maintain an invoice cadence, and produce a year-end filing pack. If you elect Auto-File, the Platform can submit your return to the IRD portal on your behalf after your explicit per-submission confirmation.
