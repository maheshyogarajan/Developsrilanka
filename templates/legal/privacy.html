1. Information We Collect

We collect data in stages, only when you need it: at signup (email, password hash, triage answers) and progressively as you use the service (name, NIC, TIN, income receipts, counterparty details, document uploads). We do not ask for NIC or TIN at signup; they are requested only when you generate your first tax-related document.

If you choose Auto-File, we additionally collect your IRD portal credentials, stored encrypted in AWS Secrets Manager with a per-customer encryption key (see §5 below). Payment card details are handled exclusively by Stripe — we never store them.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data to operate the Platform: calculate your income tax, generate service and rental agreements, maintain your invoice cadence, prepare your year-end IRD filing pack, and (if you elect Auto-File) submit your annual return to the IRD. We also use de-identified, aggregated analytics to improve the product.

We do not use your data for advertising, we do not build behavioural profiles for third-party sale, and we do not pass your data to AI model providers for general training purposes (see §7 for our processor contracts).

3. Sharing Your Information

We share your data only with the Lanka.tax service team members you connect with, and with the technical processors necessary to run the Platform (hosting, payments, email, OCR/AI features). We do not share your data with marketing networks, data brokers, or advertising platforms.

We may disclose your data where required by Sri Lankan law (e.g. a Commissioner-General request under IRA Part XI) or compelled by court order. Where legally permitted, we will notify you before complying.

4. Data Retention

We retain your tax records for seven (7) years after your last paid tax year, giving headroom above the five-year statutory retention period under section 120 of the Inland Revenue Act No. 24 of 2017. The two-year buffer covers the assessment-and-appeal window.

For tax years that we have Auto-Filed on your behalf, we retain the submission package for the full 7-year period even on account closure, because Sri Lankan tax law may require us to produce it. For Self-File years, we honour deletion requests within 21 business days. You may request deletion of identifiable account data on account closure, subject to this legitimate-interest carve-out.

5. Your Rights

Under the Sri Lankan Personal Data Protection Act, No. 9 of 2022, you have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data; to restrict processing while a dispute is open; to object to processing based on legitimate interest; to withdraw consent at any time (e.g. for IRD credentials); and to data portability (your year-end pack ZIP is your export format). To exercise these rights, email privacy@lanka.tax; we respond within 21 business days.

You can also lodge a complaint with the Sri Lankan Data Protection Authority at dpa.gov.lk.

6. Security

All data is transmitted over TLS 1.2+ with HSTS enforced. Passwords are stored hashed (bcrypt). IRD credentials are encrypted at rest in AWS Secrets Manager with a per-customer KMS key and are decrypted only at submission time by the automation runner. Production access is limited to named personnel under a least-privilege policy, with all admin actions audit-logged. Uploaded documents are MIME-checked and virus-scanned before storage.

8. Cookies

We use a session cookie (required, HttpOnly / Secure / SameSite=Lax) and a CSRF token cookie (required). We use no third-party analytics cookies, no advertising trackers, no Facebook Pixel, and no Google Analytics. Our internal product analytics is server-side and de-identified.

9. Children

FIESTA is for adult taxpayers (18+) earning foreign income. The Platform is not directed at children. If we discover we have collected data from someone under 18, we delete it promptly.

10. International Users

Our infrastructure runs primarily in the Singapore regions of Fly.io and AWS. If you access FIESTA from outside Sri Lanka, your data is processed in those jurisdictions. Cross-border transfer is covered by the contract-performance and binding-contractual-safeguards bases under PDPA Part V. We will update this section when the Data Protection Authority issues an adequacy framework.

11. Changes to This Policy

Material changes (new processor category, changed retention period, new lawful basis) will be communicated at least 14 days in advance by email and in-app banner. We log every published version so you can see what changed.
