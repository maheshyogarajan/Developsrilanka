{# STEP 1 helper: what to tell your SP #}
What to tell your Service Provider (sample script)

3 things they should know:

  1. This is a confirmation, not a new obligation. You're documenting work you already do. Signing doesn't change their tax exposure or create new liability.
  2. Signing takes ~30 seconds. They click the link, review the PDF, and type their name. No paperwork, no fees.
  3. Why you need it. Sri Lankan tax law allows expense deductions only when the relationship is documented. This is your audit-defence file.

Sample message you can copy + paste:

Hi [their name],

I'm getting my tax documentation in order for FY 2025/26. As part of that, I've prepared a short Service Agreement covering the work you do for me. You'll get an email from FIESTA shortly with the PDF + a 30-second sign-by-typing link.

It's just to confirm what we already do -- doesn't change anything between us, just helps me document it properly for the IRD. If you have any questions, reply to this email and I'll explain.

Thanks!
