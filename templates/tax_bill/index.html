{% endif %} {# ---- B13 F6.5 — IRA citations panel (always visible, zero-data safe) - #}
Why each deduction cites the law

Every deduction on your FIESTA tax bill has a specific section in the Inland Revenue Act No. 24 of 2017 behind it. The 10 categories below are the ones that apply to foreign-income earners. If the IRD ever asks, you can point to the exact statutory reference. Claim your deductions on S5 to have them reflected in your bill.

Home office rental
§6
The business-use portion of your home rent is deductible as expenditure wholly and exclusively incurred in producing your assessable income.
Internet & telecom for work
§6
The work-use share of your internet and phone bills is deductible. Apportion the annual cost by the proportion of work use.
Equipment — laptop, monitor, software
§6 + §13
Software subscriptions are fully deductible in the year paid under §6. Hardware qualifies for a capital allowance (depreciation over useful life) under §13.
Training & certifications
§6
Courses and certifications that maintain or improve skills used in your existing work are deductible. Training for a new field is not.
Service Provider fees (subcontractors)
§6 + §195
Fees paid to subcontractors who help deliver your work are deductible under §6. §195 requires disclosure of payments above statutory thresholds — FIESTA generates the documentation.
Travel for work
§6
Flights, accommodation, and transport for income-earning work are deductible. Mixed-purpose trips must be apportioned by work-days vs total days.
Professional services
§6
Accountant, tax counsel, and legal fees for business matters — including the cost of filing your return — are deductible.
Health insurance & medical
Relief schedule
Health insurance premiums for the taxpayer and qualifying dependants qualify for personal relief under the IRA First Schedule, within the statutory cap.
Solar installations
§6 + §13
Rooftop solar on premises used for income-earning work qualifies for a capital allowance under §13, capped at Rs 600,000 across the useful-life period.
Charitable donations
Relief schedule
Donations to IRD-approved charities qualify for personal relief under the IRA First Schedule, capped at 5% of taxable income. Charity must appear on the IRD approved list.
