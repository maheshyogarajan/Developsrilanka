How can you save Rs 540,000 by paying only Rs 2,500?

Because most foreign-income earners in Sri Lanka don't claim the deductions they're legally entitled to. The Inland Revenue Act §6(1) allows you to deduct expenses "wholly, exclusively and necessarily incurred" in producing your income — but you need documentation to defend each claim in an audit.

FIESTA generates that documentation: Service Agreements for your foreign clients, Rental Agreements with audit-defensible home-office language, Receipt logs with categorisation. We don't reduce your tax — we make sure you claim what you're already legally entitled to under Sri Lankan tax law.

Is this legal? Will the IRD challenge it?

Yes, it's legal. Every deduction FIESTA helps you claim is grounded in a specific section of the Inland Revenue Act — we cite the section for every claim and we attach the supporting document. No grey areas, no aggressive interpretations.

FIESTA gives you the structure most foreign-income earners can't access without paying Rs 50,000+ for individual tax counsel. The same evidence an Rs 50,000 accountant would prepare for a single audit-defence engagement — we generate it before the audit, for everyone.

Why "Rs 2,500"? And what does "free during launch" mean?

Rs 2,500 is the entry fee for FIESTA Self-File v1 (refundable within 14 days if you're not saving more than that on tax). You generate your own documents from FIESTA-provided templates, file your own return on the IRD eService portal. We don't file for you in this tier — we equip you to file correctly.

Launch period: sign up before 2026-09-01 and FIESTA is free for your first full filing — no credit card required. The Rs 2,500 entry fee begins for new sign-ups from 2026-09-01 onward. Existing launch-period customers keep their free first filing.
