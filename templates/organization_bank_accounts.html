About Bank Accounts

Bank accounts added here will be available for selection when creating invoices.

  • Set a default bank account to be pre-selected on new invoices
  • Bank account details will appear on invoices for clients to make payments
  • All team members can select from the organization's bank accounts
  • Only organization owners can add or modify bank accounts
Tips for Bank Accounts
  • Use descriptive names for your accounts (e.g., "USD Business Account", "Main LKR Account")
  • Include the branch name to help clients with bank transfers
  • For international payments, add SWIFT/BIC codes and IBAN when available
  • Set up separate accounts for different currencies if your business handles multiple currencies
